Settings

Account, security and appearance.

Account

Who you are signed in as.

Two-factor authentication

The second gate after your password. An authenticator app beats emailed codes — it keeps working when your inbox is compromised.

Vault security

An extra lock in front of your stored passwords.

Devices

Phones enrolled for fingerprint or face sign-in. The biometric check happens on the device — this server never sees it.

Backups

One archive with every project file and a full database dump, optionally pushed to Google Drive. A server-operator feature, not a per-account one — see below.

Appearance

Dark is native. Light is a separately picked palette, not an inverted one.

Sessions
